São Paulo Metro
In A Hurry? Go Under ItSão Paulo jams up above, the Metro runs below. While everyone outside looks for a way out, the campaign points in another direction, with humor. And the Metro joins the conversation of those who have no time to lose. After all, to get ahead of the traffic, you don't always have to overtake it. Sometimes you just go underneath.Role: copywriting / art direction

From Carandiru to Freedom in five minutes.

Carandiru is a São Paulo neighborhood and Metro station, also known for the city's former prison. Liberdade is another neighborhood and station; its name means “freedom” in Portuguese.

The best way to overtake the traffic? Go under it.

The Portuguese original dares the hurried driver: 'In a hurry? Go over me.' The adaptation inverts the dare: the way to overtake the traffic is to go under it - the Metro.

Every time a hole opens up in the road, we celebrate.

A hole in the road normally makes drivers complain. For the Metro, digging underground can mean extending the network — a reason to celebrate.
