PRINT CAMPAIGN / SÃO PAULO, BRAZIL

São Paulo Metro

Three headlines. Three ways to look at the city from underground.

01 / 03

From Carandiru to Freedom in five minutes.

Carandiru is a São Paulo neighborhood and Metro station, also known as the name of the city's former prison. Liberdade is another neighborhood and Metro station; in Portuguese, the name means “freedom.” The headline turns a real journey between stations into a journey from prison to freedom.

02 / 03

What's your hurry? Go underground.

The original Portuguese line plays on a sarcastic response to a pushy driver: “In a hurry? Go over me.” Replacing “over” with “under” turns a traffic provocation into an invitation to take the Metro.

03 / 03

Every time a hole appears in the road, we celebrate.

A hole in the street normally makes drivers complain. For the Metro, digging underground can mean expanding the network — turning an everyday nuisance into something worth celebrating.
