About|LinkedIn|Contact|PT / EN

Tchelo Nogueira began his creative career in fashion photography. He also explored music as a guitarist and songwriter, releasing an album of original music with his band through a record label.

He graduated in Social Communication from ESPM and completed postgraduate studies at the Royal College of Art in London. In advertising, he started as an art director before moving into copywriting, building a career spanning more than 20 years at major agencies and on independent projects, creating campaigns for brands across different industries. Along the way, his work has received recognition in national and international advertising awards, including the Cannes Lions festival.

In political communications, he has worked on the creative concepts and copywriting for electoral campaigns involving presidential, state governor and mayoral candidates.

His experience also includes developing creative projects for companies such as Roche, Itaú and Porto Seguro, combining concepts, narratives and participatory experiences in internal communications, leadership and people-development initiatives.

Alongside this work, he develops his own contemporary art practice, exploring different media and forms of expression.

His work brings together a strong visual background, strategic thinking, conceptual development and writing skills to turn communication challenges into ideas, narratives and creative solutions, from conception through creative direction and execution.
