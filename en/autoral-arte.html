CONCEPTUAL ART

ABOUT THE WORK

The work explores natural and industrial materials through experimentation, transformation and reorganization. Some pieces use charred wood, making charcoal an integral part of drawings and installations. Real or imaginary lines run through the work, connecting or separating, suggesting boundaries or pathways. Actual or implied movement creates relationships between material, form and space. Across drawings, objects and installations, the practice seeks new ways of seeing through experimentation, transformation and meaning. The work has been exhibited and recognized in solo and group exhibitions in Brazil and internationally.
